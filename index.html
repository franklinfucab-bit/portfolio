NailSense — Wireless Smart Nail Stickers for Hand Tracking

Flex-PCB · Wireless Wearables · Whole-Hand Posture Tracking

UW Ubicomp Lab · UIST 2026 Demo (Conditionally Accepted)

Unobtrusive flex-PCB smart nail stickers that wirelessly track whole-hand posture. I built and assembled the hardware — fine-pitch soldering of nail-scale assemblies, plus battery charging and handling for the wireless prototypes — and owned the fingernail attachment mechanism across three iterations. Silicone substrates rejected adhesives because of their low surface energy; adhesive on the electronics face delaminated the flex PCB under peel force during removal; moving the adhesive to the back surface eliminated PCB stress and improved conformity to the nail.

BinSense — Touchless Smart Waste Bin

ESP32-S3 · FreeRTOS · C++

A touchless waste bin built as a dual-core FreeRTOS application coordinating four sensors: PIR hand detection opens a stepper-driven lid, ultrasonic sensing tracks fill level, a photoresistor detects lid state, and temperature/humidity monitoring flags spoilage, with status on a 1602 LCD. Diagnosed a bug where concurrent ultrasonic pulseIn() measurement corrupted DHT11 bit-bang timing, and fixed it with interrupt locking. Added self-calibration and closed-loop actuation confirmation via photoresistor feedback; documented with Doxygen.

Diabetes-FCT LLM Safety Benchmark

ACM AI Letters · Under Review

A 1,020-item behavioral benchmark that separates answer selection from answer-set validation: 255 clinical seeds become paired factual-control, no-valid-option, and invalid-meta-option tasks, plus an independent 255-item fictional-premise task. An automated zero-shot pipeline (Python + regex + Ollama) stress-tested five 7–8B models. Llama-3.1-8B scored 88.2% on factual control but 23.5% when no valid option existed; Mistral-7B fell from 81.6% to 31.0% — evidence the two capabilities are behaviorally distinct.
